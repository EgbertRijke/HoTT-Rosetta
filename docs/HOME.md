# HoTT Rosetta

Read *Introduction to Homotopy Type Theory* alongside its Agda formalization.
The book text and formalized mathematics appear together, in the order of the
chapters, sections, and exercises.

Start with [Chapter 1: Dependent type theory](chapter-1-dependent-type-theory.md),
or choose a chapter from the sidebar. Search the book with the search button
or press **S**. In highlighted Agda code, click a name to jump to its definition.

The natural language text comes from the
[arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11082) of
[Egbert Rijke](https://egbertrijke.github.io/)'s
*Introduction to Homotopy Type Theory*. The formalizations are copied or adapted
from [agda-unimath](https://unimath.github.io/agda-unimath/), and Rosetta compiles
independently of that library.

Exercises include completed formalizations, pen-and-paper problems, and open
formalization tasks. See the [benchmark](BENCHMARK.md) for the unsolved and
partially solved exercises; a successful Agda build does not mean every exercise
has been solved.

See [About and contributors](README.md) for the project background, or
[Building and publishing](WEBSITE.md) to work on the website.
