## Problem statement

Consider a commuting triangle

```text
 [A]                 [B]

           [X]

Arrows:
- A --h--> B
- A --f--> X
- B --g--> X
```
with `H : f ~ g ∘ h`.

### Exercise 13.14(a)

Show that if `h` has a section, then `sec(g)` is a retract of `sec(f)`.

### Exercise 13.14(b)

Show that if `g` has a retraction, then `retr(h)` is a retract of `sec(f)`.
