## Problem statement

Consider a commuting triangle

```text
 [A]                 [B]

           [X]

Arrows:
- A --h--> B
- A --f--> X
- B --g--> X
```
with `H: f ~ g ∘ h`, and suppose that `g` is `k`-truncated.
Show that `f` is `k`-truncated if and only if `h` is `k`-truncated.
